01Day

Foundation — Brain + Chief of Staff

Build your Second Brain in Obsidian, install your Chief of Staff, and run the 12-question Business Diagnostic. By minute 80, your AI Chief of Staff knows your business cold and tells you exactly who to hire next.

4 lessons·~80 min·30 slides
  1. L 01
    The Self-Running Business System

    The pattern Chris uses to run TAM internally — a Commander in Chief at the top, an AI Chief of Staff in the middle, and Specialists doing the work. You learn the pyramid before you build it.

    ~10 min Hands-on · None — concept only
  2. L 02
    Build Your Second Brain

    Install Obsidian, run the Brain Builder Skill, and watch a Karpathy-pattern Second Brain assemble itself — 6 subfolders, an index, and a schema your future AI Employees will actually read.

    ~25 min Hands-on · Brain folder live on your machine
  3. L 03
    Install Your Chief of Staff

    One git-clone command installs the Chief of Staff template into your Brain folder. First conversation, you meet your AI orchestrator — the only AI Employee that knows everything you do.

    ~20 min Hands-on · Chief of Staff installed and greeting you
  4. L 04
    Run the Business Diagnostic

    12 questions across 4 buckets — Business, Voice, Diagnostic, Boundaries. Your Chief of Staff asks. You answer in plain English. Output: a Hire Recommendations memo with your next 2 AI Employees ranked by impact.

    ~25 min Hands-on · Hire Recommendations memo in your Brain

    Hire #2 via /hire-employee

    Watch Chief of Staff generate a complete Specialist folder — SKILL.md, voice rules, boundary rules, starter skills — all read from your Brain. Four steps. Seven minutes. One Specialist ready to run.
